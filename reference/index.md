  : NYC subway routes
- [`nyc_subway_routes_sf`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_routes_sf.md)

## Subway stops

- [`nyc_subway_stops_sf`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_stops_sf.md)
  : NYC subway stops
- [`nyc_subway_stops_parent_sf`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_stops_parent_sf.md)
  : NYC subway parent stations
- [`nyc_subway_stops_platform_sf`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_stops_platform_sf.md)

## Transfers

Transfer relationships between stops.

- [`nyc_subway_transfers_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_transfers_df.md)
  : NYC subway transfers

## Station complexes

Bridge tables between MTA `station_complex_id` (used by the ridership
packages) and GTFS parent `stop_id` and `route_id`.

- [`nyc_subway_complexes_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_complexes_df.md)
  : NYC subway station complexes
- [`nyc_subway_complexes_sf`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_complexes_sf.md)
  : NYC subway station complexes (sf)
- [`nyc_subway_complex_stops_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_complex_stops_df.md)
  : NYC subway complex-to-parent-stop mapping
- [`nyc_subway_complex_routes_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_complex_routes_df.md)
  : NYC subway complex-to-route service map
- [`nyc_subway_stop_routes_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_stop_routes_df.md)
  : NYC subway parent-stop route service map

- [`nyc_subway_stops_offset_sf`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycroutes/reference/nyc_subway_stops_offset_sf.md)
